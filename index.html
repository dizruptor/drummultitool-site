Drum Multitool

3 in 1 for drummers

A drummer metronome, a drum kit tuner and a pattern editor that writes real drum notation, in one app. Not a general music app with a drum mode: every part of it is built only for drummers.

Drummer metronome

A tempo dial with tap tempo, 1–12 beats, 2–8 notes per beat with swing, accents and muted beats, voice count and gap bars, on a click that never drifts.

Drum kit tuner

Finds a drum's true fundamental, not an overtone. Tune the whole kit by intervals, top and resonant heads, with reference tones and a lugs mode.

Pattern editor

Tap in grooves, see them as drum notation, hear them on recorded rock and jazz kits. Flams, drags, rolls, tuplets and MusicXML import and export.

Privacy policy

Effective 21 November 2026

Drum Multitool is an app for drummers: a drum kit tuner, a metronome and a pattern editor. It's made by Symbiotic Systems ("I", "me").

In short: the app doesn't collect, store on any server, share or sell any personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Everything you do in the app stays on your phone.

Microphone

The tuner needs the microphone to hear your drums. Android asks for permission the first time you open the tuner.

  • The microphone is used only while the tuner is on screen and the app is open. It stops when you switch to another tool or leave the app.
  • The sound is analysed on the phone, as it arrives, to measure pitch. It is not recorded, saved or sent anywhere.
  • You can withdraw the permission at any time in Android's settings. The metronome and the pattern editor work without it.

What the app keeps on your phone

The app saves these in its own storage on your phone, so they're there next time:

  • your settings (metronome, tuner, colours);
  • your kit's measured and target pitches;
  • your patterns;
  • spoken count words ("one", "two"…), made once by your phone's text-to-speech engine;
  • the date the free trial started, and whether Pro has been bought.

None of this leaves the phone. It's deleted when you uninstall the app, or when you clear its storage in Android's settings. Android's own device backup may include app data, under your Google account's backup settings; that is between you and Google.

Files you import or export

When you import or export a MusicXML file, Android's file picker opens. The app reads only the file you choose, or writes only where you choose to save it.

Purchases

The optional Pro upgrade is bought through Google Play. Google handles the payment under Google's privacy policy. The app learns only whether Pro has been bought; I never see your name, email address or payment details.

Other permissions

  • Notifications: while the metronome or a pattern plays, Android shows a notification with a Stop button. This is what lets it keep playing with the screen off.
  • Foreground media playback: also for playing with the screen off.

Children

The app isn't directed at children under 13, and it collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.

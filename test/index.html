Drummers wanted

Test Drum Multitool before it launches

I'm a drummer that codes so I built my dream drum buddy app: a drummer metronome, a drum kit tuner and a pattern editor that writes real drum notation, in one app.

Before it goes on Google Play, I'm looking for drummers with an Android phone to try it for two weeks.

Drummer metronome

A tempo dial with tap tempo, 1–12 beats, 2–8 notes per beat with swing, rests inside the beat for gallops, accents and muted beats, a spoken count and gap bars. The click is timed to the sample, so it never drifts, and it keeps going with the screen off.

Drum kit tuner

Made for drums, not guitars: it finds the drum's real note, not a louder overtone. Tune the whole kit by intervals, top and resonant heads, with reference tones to hear the target, and a lugs mode to even out the head.

Pattern editor

A growing library of built-in grooves, practice patterns and classic grooves, or tap in your own and see them as proper drum notation. Hear them on recorded rock and jazz kits, with flams, drags, buzz rolls, ghost notes and tuplets. Practise along: loop any bars at any tempo, with swing. Import or export MusicXML.

What testing means

  • You need an Android phone and the Google account you use in its Play Store. (Sorry, no iPhone version yet.)
  • I send you an invite link. You tap it, join the test, and install the app from the Play Store like any other app. It's free.
  • Keep it installed for 14 days. Google requires this before a new app can be published.
  • Use whichever tools are useful to you, whether that's keeping time, tuning your kit, or writing grooves and practising along with them, and tell me what you think: what works, what's confusing, what's missing. Optional, but it's what makes the app better.

How to join

Email drummultitool@gmail.com with the subject Tester, and include the Gmail or Google account address you use in the Play Store on your Android phone.

Your address is used only to invite you to the test. The app has no ads and no account, and it collects no data: see the privacy policy.
